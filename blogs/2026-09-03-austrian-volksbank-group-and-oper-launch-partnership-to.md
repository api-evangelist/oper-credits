Vienna, 3 September 2026 – Oper, Europe’s leading provider of technology for digital mortgage lending, today announced a partnership with VOLKSBANK WIEN AG, which has been the central organization of the Association of Volksbanks (Volksbanken-Verbund) since July 2015 and is one of Austria’s most significant banking groups. The partnership aims to accelerate the digital transformation of financing processes across all 236 branches nationwide, which together serve nearly 1 million customers.
